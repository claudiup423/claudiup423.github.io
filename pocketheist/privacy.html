Pocket Heist - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Pocket Heist ("the game") is made by Cronapt ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your game progress (cash, unlocks, settings, achievements) is stored only on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game bonuses. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game which items you own so they can be restored.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with Google as described above, for advertising and payment processing.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Data processed by Google is handled under Google's own retention policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available in the game and at the address listed on our Google Play page.
